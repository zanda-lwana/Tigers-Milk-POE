ABOUT TIGER'S MILK

Learn more about Tiger's Milk Moffett on Main.

OUR RESTAURANT

Tiger's Milk is a well-known South African restaurant and bar that offers gourmet burgers, wood-fired pizzas, steaks, cocktails and entertainment.

Tiger's Milk Moffett on Main is located at Moffett on Main Lifestyle Centre in Greenshields Park, Gqeberha.

The restaurant provides customers with a modern dining experience in a vibrant and welcoming atmosphere.

OUR HISTORY

Tiger's Milk has grown into a popular South African restaurant and bar brand. It is known for its relaxed atmosphere, quality food and social environment.

The Moffett on Main branch provides a local dining experience for people living in and visiting Gqeberha.

OUR MISSION

To create memorable dining experiences by serving high-quality food and drinks while providing outstanding customer service in a fun and welcoming atmosphere.

OUR VISION

To be a leading casual dining restaurant in Gqeberha by combining exceptional food, outstanding service and innovative digital solutions that improve the customer experience.

WHO WE SERVE

  • Families
  • University students
  • Young professionals
  • Business professionals
  • Tourists visiting Gqeberha
  • Groups of friends
